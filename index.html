Our Story

Engineering Excellence
Since 1969


Chetan Engineering Corporation was founded in 1969 in Vadodara, Gujarat, with a singular mission — to deliver high-quality, durable industrial gates and metal fabrication solutions to the businesses and industries of India. What started as a modest workshop has grown, over five decades, into one of the most trusted names in industrial gate manufacturing in the region.

Our state-of-the-art in-house manufacturing facility in Nizampura, Vadodara allows us to maintain complete quality control from raw material sourcing to final installation. Every product that leaves our workshop carries the hallmark of precision craftsmanship and structural reliability that our clients have come to depend on.

We serve a diverse range of sectors — from large-scale factories and warehouses to commercial complexes, educational institutions and residential projects — bringing the same dedication to quality and customisation to every engagement.

What We Make

Our Product Range

From collapsible gates to heavy-duty industrial doors — we manufacture a comprehensive range of access control and enclosure solutions, all engineered for durability and built to your specifications.

Collapsible Gates

Retractable accordion-style gates for shops, warehouses and industrial premises. Space-efficient, robust and available in multiple configurations.

Sliding Gates

Heavy-duty sliding gates for factories, industrial facilities and large commercial premises. Engineered for smooth, reliable operation in high-traffic environments.

Swing Gates

Traditional and modern swing gates for residential, commercial and institutional entry points. Available in single and double-leaf designs with decorative options.

Automatic Gates

Motorised automated gate systems with remote and sensor control. Ideal for modern factories, gated communities and commercial complexes in Vadodara and Gujarat.

Rolling Shutters

Galvanised steel and aluminium rolling shutters for shops, showrooms and industrial units. Manual, spring-operated and motorised variants available.

Industrial Doors

Heavy-duty steel industrial doors for warehouses, loading docks and manufacturing plants. Designed for high durability and long service life under demanding conditions.

Boom Barriers

Automatic boom barriers for parking lots, toll plazas and controlled access zones. Fast, reliable and compatible with RFID, remote and push-button controls.

Railings & Staircases

Custom MS and SS railings, handrails and staircase structures for industrial, commercial and residential projects. Precision-fabricated for safety and aesthetics.

Custom Metal Fabrication

Bespoke structural steel and mild steel fabrication to your drawings and specifications. From brackets and frames to complex structural components — we build it.

What We Offer

Our Services

End-to-end solutions — from design and fabrication to professional installation and ongoing maintenance — all delivered by our experienced in-house team.

In-House Manufacturing

All products are manufactured at our own facility in Vadodara, ensuring consistent quality, faster turnaround and competitive pricing without middlemen.

Custom Fabrication

We fabricate to your exact drawings, dimensions and finish requirements. Structural steel, MS sections and special profiles — all custom-made in our workshop.

Professional Installation

Our skilled installation teams handle on-site work across Vadodara and Gujarat, ensuring every gate, shutter and barrier is fitted precisely and safely.

Maintenance Services

Scheduled maintenance programs to keep your gates, shutters and barriers in peak operating condition. We service all products we supply and install.

Repair & Refurbishment

Fast, effective repair services for damaged or ageing gates and shutters. We refurbish and restore existing installations to extend their operational life.

On-Site Consultation

Free site visits and expert consultations to recommend the right product, size and configuration for your specific premises and operational requirements.
